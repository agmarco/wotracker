## 🏁 SUNDAY OCTOBER 18 — RACE DAY

**Target: 9:00–9:15/mile | Finish: ~3:56–4:02**

| Miles | Pace | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| 1–3 | 9:30–9:45/mile | Start conservative. Let everyone go. |
| 4–13 | 9:00–9:15/mile | Lock in and hold steady. |
| 14–18 | 9:00–9:15/mile | Stay controlled. This is where races are lost. |
| 19–23 | Hold or push slightly | Your training kicks in here. |
| 24–26.2 | Everything you have left | Empty the tank. |

**Fueling on race day:**
- Gel at miles 5, 9, 13, 16, 18 and 21
- Always take gels with water
- Small sips of water every mile — don't wait until you're thirsty

**Remember:** The race starts at mile 18. Everything before that is just getting there.
