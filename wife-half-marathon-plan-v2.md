# Half Marathon Training Plan
**Goal:** Complete a half marathon (13.1 miles) | **Timeline:** 17 weeks | **Days/week:** 3
**Current fitness:** 10K at 10:30/mile

---

## How This Plan Works
Every run in this plan is easy pace — there is no tempo work, no speed work, no pace pressure.
The only goal is gradually extending distance until 13.1 miles feels comfortable.

**All runs:** 11:00–11:30/mile | Treadmill: 5.2–5.5 mph

Spread your 3 runs across the week — for example Monday, Wednesday, Saturday.
The long run should always be your last run of the week with at least one rest day before it.
Bootcamp on Tuesday and Friday stays as is — no changes needed.

## RACE WEEK — Week 17

**Run 1 — Shakeout**
- 2 miles easy at 11:00–11:30/mile
- Relaxed. No effort at all.

**Run 2 — Optional**
- 20 minute very easy jog or walk
- If legs feel heavy, skip it entirely.

**Race Day 🏁**
- Goal: finish strong and enjoy every mile
- Pace: 11:00–11:30/mile — start here and hold it
- Fuel: gel at miles 4, 7 and 10
- Walk through water stations — small sips every mile
- Miles 1–3 will feel easy. Do not speed up.
- Mile 10 is where it gets real. Shorten your stride, keep breathing steady.
- You've done the work. Trust it.

---

## Key Reminders Throughout Training

1. **Every run is easy.** If you can't hold a full conversation, slow down.
2. **The long run is the most important run each week.** If you can only do one run, make it the long run.
3. **Recovery weeks are not optional.** Weeks 4 and 8 are intentionally shorter — do not add miles.
4. **The taper will feel like too little.** That's exactly right. Trust the process.
5. **Bootcamp stays as is.** It directly supports your running — don't stop.
6. **If you miss a run:** one missed run per week is fine, just continue. If you miss a full week, repeat that week before moving on.
